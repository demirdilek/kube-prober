# ADR 0010: Alertmanager Multi-Channel Routing & Anti-Alert-Fatigue

* **Status:** Accepted
* **Date:** 2026-07-21

## Context

When monitoring Kubernetes infrastructure and target services with `Kube Prober` , firing alerts must reach on-call engineers quickly and with high actionable context.

Unfiltered, noisy, or poorly routed alerts cause severe operational problems:

* **Alert Fatigue:** On-call operators become desensitized to frequent, non-actionable notifications, increasing the risk of missing critical outages.
* **Lack of Context:** Notifications without diagnostic hints force operators to spend critical time digging through logs and dashboards during incidents.
* **Notification Flooding:** Flapping metrics can cause hundreds of duplicate alerts during temporary network spikes.

## Decision

We decided to design a multi-tiered **Alertmanager routing architecture** (`prom-stack-values.yaml`) featuring severity-based channel dispatching (Slack & Pushover), intelligent grouping, and automated diagnostic hint injection.

## Options Considered

* **Single Channel Setup (Slack Only):** *Rejected because* non-critical warnings and high-urgency system outages pollute the same channel, risking missed critical incidents during off-hours.
* **Raw Un-grouped Alerting:** *Rejected because* triggering individual notifications per failing pod creates massive notification storms during cluster-wide network degradations.
* **Multi-Tiered Routing with Severity Classification (Chosen):** *Selected because* it routes non-critical audit trails to Slack (`#alerts`) while escalating critical outages to high-priority mobile push notifications (Pushover). It also suppresses noisy internal cluster watchdogs (e.g., k3s/k3d internal Watchdog) and groups alerts by category and severity.

## Consequences

### Positive

* **Actionable Notification Templates:** Slack and Pushover notifications automatically attach the `summary`, `category`, and diagnostic `hint` generated by the SRE error categorization engine, enabling fast initial triage directly from the alert.
* **Alert Fatigue Prevention:**
  * Grouping alerts by `alertname`, `severity`, and `category` consolidates multi-pod failures into single cohesive messages.
  * Extended repeat intervals (`repeat_interval: 12h`) prevent constant chatter for known ongoing issues.
* **Noisy Alert Suppression:** Uninformative infrastructure heartbeats (like Kubernetes `Watchdog`) are explicitly silenced via the `null` receiver.
* **GitOps & Secret Management Integration:** Alertmanager webhook endpoints, Slack URLs, and Pushover API tokens are mounted securely from Kubernetes secrets generated via environment files (`scripts/deploy-prometheus.sh`).

### Negative / Trade-offs

* **Secret Dependency:** Requires maintaining valid webhook URLs and user keys in local `.env` files or secret stores before executing infrastructure deployments.
* **Interval Latency:** Group wait times (`group_wait: 10s`, `group_interval: 5m`) introduce a slight delay before initial alert aggregation to prevent notification spamming.
